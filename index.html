Energy and sustainability

Pneumatic vs electric

Both have a place. Know when to offer each.

Pneumatic

Benefits

  • Simple and robust
  • Low cost to buy
  • Fast, simple end-to-end movement
  • Easy to install and maintain
  • Copes well with harsh, dirty or wet areas

Limitations

  • Best suited to set end positions
  • Compressed air costs energy, and leaks waste it
  • Less control of speed and force along the stroke
  • Needs an air supply and air preparation

Electric

Benefits

  • Precise positioning to many points
  • Speed, force and position are programmable
  • Uses energy mainly when moving
  • Quiet and clean
  • Feedback and diagnostics

Limitations

  • Higher initial cost
  • More set-up: motor, controller, cabling and software
  • Needs more know-how to commission
  • Can be more than a simple job needs

Ask which jobs only need end positions, and which need precise control.
